The transcript still comes from the model above, which is the complete one. gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize runs alongside it purely to tell who is speaking when — on its own it loses about 40% of the words, so its text is discarded. Speaker turns are mapped onto the full transcript, and each change of speaker starts a new paragraph. Doubles the cost and the time.

Transcribes the audio a second time with the parts cut in different places, then folds in any sentence the first pass did not have. On hard audio — people talking over each other, background noise — one pass caught 17 of 24 sentences and the two together caught 21. Doubles cost and time; untick it for easy recordings.

For bilingual or interpreted recordings: each paragraph is labelled with the language spoken in it, so a passage repeated in another language simply becomes its own paragraph. Leave Language on auto-detect for these.
